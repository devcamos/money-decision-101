## Removed or rewritten claims

The following legacy claims were removed or materially narrowed because they were unsupported, overly absolute, US-specific, or recommendation-like:

- `401(k)` and `IRA` references.
- "Join thousands of beginners" social proof.
- "What to invest in first" recommendation wording.
- "Markets ... trend upward over decades" as an absolute claim.
- "A low-cost total market index fund is a great starting point for most people."
- Generic 20% credit-card comparison phrased as a universal rule.
- "A 1% difference in fees can cost hundreds of thousands" without assumptions.
- "The best time to start was yesterday" urgency language.

## Page invariants

1. The page is clearly scoped to UK educational content.
2. Material financial concepts display or link to official UK authority sources.
3. The page never recommends a named security, fund, provider, account provider or portfolio allocation.
4. The page does not use unsupported social proof or performance guarantees.
5. Money Decision 101 is the site entry. Investing 101 is the downstream guide after an investing-readiness result, and direct educational access remains available. Investing 101 must not send people back through Money Decision as if that were how to enter this page, and it must not give an investment recommendation.
6. Authority citations must not imply authority endorsement of this site or its author.
7. Martin Lewis / MoneySavingExpert must not appear in Official guidance used.

## Review cadence

Review at least every 6 months, and earlier when any cited authority materially updates its relevant guidance or when UK tax-wrapper rules change.

## Interaction boundaries

Every new question checks a definition or misconception. Incorrect answers produce explanations and permit continuing. Completion says the learner has explored the foundations, never that they qualify to invest. No personal financial inputs, fund recommendations, rankings, allocations, forecasts or action signals are generated. Readiness policy is unchanged.

Notion research notes informed topic discovery only. Their allocations, return assumptions, personal investment rules and specific holdings are not consumer-page evidence.
