# Investing 101 Ontology v3

Version 3.0.0 · Content review date: 2026-10-16 · Supersedes v2 for the current guide.

The educational graph expands from 13 to 18 concepts: regional markets, indices, world-index coverage, S&P 500 as a benchmark example, and ETF structure. See ontology-v3.json and evidence-registry-v2.md. Version 2 files remain historical.

Direct /investing access remains available. Money Decision still offers the learning route only after its existing readiness flow. OPA policy 1.0.0 is untouched. Practice questions never evaluate personal suitability or unlock investments.

Named indices explain coverage, not what to buy. No fund tickers, provider picks, return forecasts, allocations or personal recommendations. Europe is not interchangeable with EU/eurozone; Asia is not one uniform market. World-index names do not guarantee universal coverage. ETF structure is distinct from passive strategy and diversification.

Index providers are primary sources for their own benchmark definitions, not regulators or independent endorsements. The SEC source is US educational material used only for ETF structure, not UK protections, tax or suitability. No live weights or performance tables are copied. UK guidance continues to govern the general consumer boundaries.
